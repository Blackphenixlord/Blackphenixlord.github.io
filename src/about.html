My name is Joshua Collado, and I'm a dual enrollment student at UTRGV through the Science Academy — studying engineering, computer science, and the future. I'm from South Texas, and I'm on a mission to use technology to make life better, smarter, and more collaborative.

I've had the privilege of working with NASA on an Inventory Chain System that granted me 2 visits to the Houston HQ, built a Virtual Reality training experience with Los Alamos National Laboratory, developed a city website with RECON Technologies, and co-created a STEM sandbox for elementary schools with Nerdvana.

Right now I'm designing Evie — a personal AI assistant that connects Android and Windows into a unified home automation network. My goal is to become a software engineer and an AI developer who builds systems that actually change how people live and work.
