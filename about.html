01 · introduction

My name is Joshua Collado, and I'm a high school student enrolled at UTRGV through the Science Academy Dual Enrollment program in South Texas. I study engineering and computer science with a single goal: use technology to make life better, smarter, and more connected for the people around me. I've had the privilege of working alongside researchers at NASA and Los Alamos National Laboratory, building real systems that solve real problems — from inventory management workflows to virtual reality training simulations. I don't just write code; I think in systems, build with purpose, and iterate until something actually works. Right now I'm designing Evie, a personal AI assistant that bridges Android and Windows into a unified home automation network — because the future I want to live in doesn't exist yet, so I'm building it myself.
